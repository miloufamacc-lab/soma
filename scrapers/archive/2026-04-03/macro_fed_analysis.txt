Federal Reserve Rate Decision Analysis — April 2026

The Federal Reserve held rates steady at 4.25% as expected. Chair Powell's press conference signaled a cautious approach, noting that inflation remains above the 2% target at 2.8% CPI.

Key takeaways from the FOMC meeting:
- The dot plot suggests 2 rate cuts in 2026, down from 3 projected in January
- Powell emphasized "data dependency" and avoiding premature easing
- Labor market shows early signs of softening with NFP at 142K vs 180K expected
- Quantitative tightening continues at $60B/month pace

The PMI data tells a mixed story — manufacturing ISM at 48.2 (contractionary) while services ISM remains expansionary at 52.1. This divergence has historically preceded regime transitions in 60% of cases.
