Taiwan Strait Tensions — Weekly Update

China's PLA conducted naval exercises in the Taiwan Strait for the third consecutive week, with 47 aircraft and 12 vessels detected in the median line area. This represents a significant escalation from the baseline of 15-20 aircraft per week observed in January.

The US responded by deploying the USS Ronald Reagan carrier group to the Philippine Sea, while Japan announced increased surveillance flights over the East China Sea.

Semiconductor supply chain implications:
- TSMC advanced node production (3nm, 5nm) remains concentrated in Taiwan
- US CHIPS Act facilities in Arizona are 18 months from production readiness
- Samsung's Pyeongtaek expansion targets 2027 for advanced GAA nodes
- Any disruption to TSMC would create a 6-12 month global chip shortage

Transmission channels to markets:
- Energy: minimal direct impact (Taiwan not a major oil route)
- Semiconductors: CRITICAL — 65% of global advanced logic chips
- Risk-off: VIX likely to spike 15-25% on any kinetic escalation
- Safe havens: Gold, USD, JGBs would benefit
