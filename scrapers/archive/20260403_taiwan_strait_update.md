# Taiwan Strait Tensions — April 3, 2026

## Summary
China conducted military exercises near Taiwan following a state visit by a senior US diplomat. The exercises involved naval vessels and aircraft in waters east of Taiwan.

Key developments:
- 12 PLA naval vessels detected in Taiwan Strait
- Semiconductor supply chain risk elevated — TSMC production unaffected so far
- VIX spiked to 22 on the news before settling at 19
- Japan Self-Defense Forces on elevated alert
- US carrier group repositioned to Philippine Sea

## Market Impact
- Risk-off sentiment in Asian equities
- Safe haven flows into USD, JPY, gold
- Semiconductor stocks ($NVDA, $TSM, $ASML) sold off 3-5%
- Oil prices rose on strait closure risk

This represents a severity 3 escalation from the baseline 2 for the Taiwan Strait region.
