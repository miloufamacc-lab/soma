INVESTMENT THESIS DISCIPLINE — Personal Reflection

After reviewing my trading journal from Q1 2026, I notice a clear pattern:
every time I deviated from my pre-committed thesis rules, the outcome was worse
than if I had stuck to the framework.

Key lessons:
- Position sizing discipline matters more than entry timing
- Drawdown management through regime-based exposure is working
- The conviction framework needs to weight base rates over recent data
- Behavioral bias awareness (recency, anchoring) has prevented 3 bad trades
- Contrarian positions that align with thesis outperform reactionary trades

The compounding advantage of avoiding large drawdowns continues to be the
single most important insight. A 30% drawdown requires 43% gain to recover.
Risk management is alpha generation in disguise.

Strategy principle: if the thesis hasn't changed, the position shouldn't change.
