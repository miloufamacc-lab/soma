## RED TEAM (Phase 2.5 — top-5 by impact)

### C1 — "CEO's job is promotion" — COUNTER
- Counter-argument: Causality runs the other way. For early-stage founders pre-PMF, the highest-ROI activity is talking to users and iterating product — NOT performative promotion. Paul Graham's "Do things that don't scale" and Brian Chesky's Airbnb-founder-as-photographer story both argue the opposite: founder-led sales/feedback, not broadcast promotion, is the pre-PMF job. Only post-PMF does promotion dominate.
- Evidence: Airbnb's first 100 customers came from founders going door-to-door photographing apartments — zero podcast appearances. Stripe/Collison brothers rarely promoted in public until the product was clearly working.
- Source: PG essays ("Do Things That Don't Scale", 2013); Chesky interviews.
- Steelman strength: **STRONG**. Pre-PMF vs. post-PMF distinction is absent from Jonathan's framing.
- Confidence adjustment: C1 -0.05 → 0.65.

### C2 — Promoter Blueprint — COUNTER
- Counter-argument: The framework is borrowed, not novel. Russell Brunson's "Dotcom Secrets" (2015) describes this same funnel; ActiveCampaign/Infusionsoft orthodoxy pre-dates it by a decade. The incremental insight ("holding pattern" label, 90% loopback) is branding, not new science. Also: the 90% loopback figure is unsourced and likely optimistic — typical re-engagement rates across industries are 15-30%, and list decay is real.
- Evidence: Mailchimp industry benchmarks show ~20% email open rates, 2-5% click rates, with 5-25% annual list decay; "90% back to holding" implies a retention rate most operators don't see.
- Source: Mailchimp Email Marketing Benchmarks; Brunson, "Dotcom Secrets" (2015).
- Steelman strength: **MODERATE** — framework is valid, but novelty and retention math are overstated.
- Confidence adjustment: C2 no change (framework is sound; only retention claim softens).

### C3 — "AI as procrastination machines" — COUNTER
- Counter-argument: Some of the "optimization" actually compounds. Teams that build internal tools early (Stripe, Shopify, Figma) have documented productivity moats from investing in internal systems during zero-revenue phases. The diagnostic "are you making money from this" at the *activity* level misses compounding infrastructure value at the *portfolio* level.
- Evidence: Stripe's internal tooling team was larger than customer-facing eng for years; paid off in deployment velocity.
- Source: Stripe engineering blog, various.
- Steelman strength: **MODERATE**. True for top-quartile technical teams. Weak for solo non-technical founders (Jonathan's actual audience).
- Confidence adjustment: C3 no change — Jonathan's framing is correct for his stated audience.

### C4 — Funnel math (5K → 200 → 12) — COUNTER
- Counter-argument: Internal consistency ≠ external validity. The 4% email→webinar rate is high for cold lists (industry median 1-2%); 6% webinar→sale is very high (industry median 2-5%). Either (a) AJ&Smart's newsletter is exceptionally warm due to 14-year brand, or (b) numbers are directional estimates rather than measured averages. Either way, benchmark generalization is dangerous for audiences with cold/mixed lists.
- Evidence: BigMarker, Demio, ON24 industry webinar benchmarks show 35-45% registration-to-attend rate but 2-5% attend-to-purchase for cold audiences.
- Source: Demio State of Webinars 2024.
- Steelman strength: **STRONG**. Audience generalization of these numbers will disappoint.
- Confidence adjustment: C4 -0.05 → 0.50 (applied).

### C6 — "Abundance over efficiency" — COUNTER
- Counter-argument: For capital-constrained or early-stage teams, "scale up like crazy" is exactly wrong — it extends runway consumption. Many post-ZIRP (2022-2024) failures came from over-hiring on AI hype. The "abundance" framing implicitly assumes persistent revenue to feed the team; for pre-PMF or revenue-unstable startups, leanness is still the play.
- Evidence: 2022-2024 tech layoffs (Layoffs.fyi: ~500K tech layoffs); YC's repeated advice to pre-PMF founders is "default alive, not default dead."
- Source: Layoffs.fyi; YC Startup School.
- Steelman strength: **STRONG**. Audience-dependent: advice works for Jonathan's post-PMF agency, harmful for pre-PMF vibecoders (his stated audience).
- Confidence adjustment: C6 -0.05 → 0.55 (applied).
