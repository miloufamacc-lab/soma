## Q: What assumptions underpin these claims?

- **$300T thesis:** Capital allocators accept BTC volatility as the universal hurdle rate. *If false:* liability-matching pools never migrate; STRC stays niche.
- **Squeeze sequence:** STRC scales 30-100x without yield compression; no superior credit instrument; reg framework holds. *If yields compress:* retail demand falls below break-even.
- **US regulator alignment:** Named regulators stay 4+ years; non-US defers. *If turnover:* SEC re-files cases, OCC reverses guidance.
- **TAM $300-400T:** Allocator preferences fluid; FDIC/regulatory-capital filters don't bite. *If filters bite:* addressable subset is $15-20T.
- **Banking adoption 2026:** FASB ASU 2023-08 + Basel relief converts pilots to production. *If treasurers wait:* adoption delayed to 2028+.
- **BTC/share 7y doubling:** Capital markets remain open to MSTR issuance; BTC remains preferred unit of account.
